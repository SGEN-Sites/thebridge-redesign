Who we are

The Bridge is a partnership between Havoc of Mobb Deep and NDICA. Havoc grew up in Queensbridge and helped define the sound of New York hip-hop. NDICA is a nonprofit working for a cannabis industry that serves the communities hit hardest by the War on Drugs. Put those together and you get a dispensary rooted in culture and built for the community.

What's inside

The shelves cover flower, pre-rolls, vapes, edibles, concentrates, beverages, wellness and lifestyle, from glassware to merch. What's on the menu changes, so the online store is the place to see it.

ASTORIA

  • More than a counter

    We open the floor. Recently at The Bridge: brand pop-ups, a meet and greet with Havoc and a free all-levels mat Pilates class. Brands, artists, wellness practitioners and community groups from the neighborhood can bring us an idea too.

    See what's on at Events, or get on The List.

  • Order online

    Not coming through today? Order online at store.thebridgecannabis.com. We deliver. Have your ID ready when it arrives: New York requires a check at delivery.

  • Before you come through

    It's 21 and over, with a valid ID. First time? The Dispensary Guide covers the whole visit, including how to check any shop on the state's list, ours included. Questions? Get in touch or call 917-832-7420.
