OUR STORY

WELCOME TO THE BRIDGE DISPENSARY

The Bridge Dispensary was born from a partnership between Havoc from Mobb Deep, legendary hip-hop artist and entrepreneur, and NDICA. Together, the venture reimagines cannabis retail by blending cultural leadership, community empowerment, and a curated premium product experience.

Connecting the cannabis industry with underserved communities, championing diversity, social equity, and economic opportunity.
