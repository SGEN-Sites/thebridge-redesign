How to Shop a Dispensary Without Overspending

The priciest jar isn't always the strongest and the cheapest isn't always the better buy. A little math before you reach the counter goes a long way.

On this page

  1. Know what the register adds
  2. Compare by the unit, not the sticker
  3. Stop paying for the biggest number
  4. Bulk only pays if you finish it
  5. Don't save money on the wrong thing
  6. Go in ready
  7. Sources

Set your number before you walk in. Not after you've smelled three jars and read the whole menu. Most of the money that leaks at the counter comes down to a few pieces of math nobody taught you, so here they are.

Know what the register adds

Adult-use sales in New York carry retail taxes totaling 13%: 9% for the state and 4% local. Cannabis products themselves are exempt from regular sales tax. So build it in. If tax is added on top, a $50 order comes to $56.50. Ask whether the price you're looking at already includes it, and take the receipt. The state's sample receipt breaks the taxes out line by line.

Compare by the unit, not the sticker

A sticker price hides the size. One bit of division fixes that.

  • Flower: price per gram. An eighth is 3.5 grams, a quarter is 7, a half is 14, an ounce is 28. A $35 eighth works out to $10 a gram. A $60 quarter is about $8.57.
  • Edibles and drinks: price per milligram of THC. Multiply milligrams per serving by servings in the package, then divide the price by that. A $20 package with 10 servings of 5 milligrams costs 40 cents a milligram.
  • Vapes: price per gram of oil, comparing the same size and type. Then think about the hardware you're buying along with it.

Those prices are made up to show the math. Run it on the real menu before you come in.

Stop paying for the biggest number

THC percentage is the easiest thing to shop by and the least useful on its own. The state is blunt about it: a higher percentage does not necessarily mean a better experience, because terpenes and other cannabinoids contribute too. Its guide also asks a better question: what is the minimum effective amount of cannabis for your body? The less you need, the longer your money lasts and the lower your odds of overdoing it. Nobody selling you anything will lead with that, so we will.

Bulk only pays if you finish it

Bigger sizes can look cheaper by the gram, but flower doesn't wait for you. In a 2020 storage study, terpene levels fell under every condition tested within four months, and room temperature caused the largest changes in cannabinoids over a year. If an ounce will sit for months, you're paying for a smell that's already gone. There's a legal ceiling as well: adults may possess up to 3 ounces of cannabis and 24 grams of concentrate outside the home.

Don't save money on the wrong thing

An unlicensed seller may quote you a lower price, and nobody is checking that seller's work. The state warns that unregulated products may contain heavy metals and pesticides and have been found listing inaccurate THC. Some even copy the universal symbol. So-called gifting, where cannabis comes free with a sticker or a t-shirt you pay for, is illegal. Look for the Dispensary Verification Tool at the storefront and scan it.

Go in ready

  1. Bring valid government ID that shows your age and photo.
  2. Browse the menu first and shortlist two or three things.
  3. Keep notes on what worked. It's the cheapest upgrade there is, because it stops you from rebuying a miss. The state's guide has journal pages for this.
  4. Ask questions. The state itself calls budtenders a resource, and there's nothing wrong with naming your budget out loud.
  5. Keep your order number. Our Contact page explains that returns and exchanges are handled case by case.

Not sure what a product's numbers mean yet? Start with the label, or head to the shop and try the math on something real.
